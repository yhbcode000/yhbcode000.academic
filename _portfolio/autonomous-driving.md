Research Assistant at the State Key Laboratory of Automotive Safety and Energy, Tsinghua University. Led a 10-person team developing perception systems for autonomous driving.

**Abnormal Information Flow Detection**: Analyzed information flow irregularities in neural networks through layer-wise entropy stability framework, achieving two orders of magnitude improvement in real-time anomaly detection.

**Multi-Weather Adaptation**: Combined specific and shared parameter learning for robust image restoration across varying environmental conditions.

Co-authored 2 patents and 4 papers. Contributed to NSFC grant on multimodal fusion joint encoding for intelligent driving perception safety.
