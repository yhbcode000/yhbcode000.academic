Developing an embodied robot interaction framework based on multimodal large model agents, integrating unified vision-language-action representations. Implementing "one-sentence-multiple-step" long-horizon task closed-loop with rich dialogue experiences.

![ChatPiano Architecture](/images/chatpiano-arch.webp)

The system uses a non-blocking multi-threaded architecture with parallel tool servers and notification threads for real-time execution.

![China National Symphony Orchestra at MBZUAI](/images/chatpiano-orchestra.webp)

Performed alongside the China National Symphony Orchestra during their UAE arts festival visit at MBZUAI, demonstrating AI-human collaborative performance.
