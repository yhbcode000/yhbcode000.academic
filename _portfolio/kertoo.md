KerToo is an AI and robotics startup redefining human-animal-machine relationships through embodied intelligence. We design AI companions that enrich pet care through emotion, interactivity, and adaptive intelligence.

Presented at the 2024 China Pet Industry Conference and secured seed funding. The project combines advanced embodied AI with thoughtful design to create meaningful experiences — using AI to inspire affection, empathy, and companionship between pets and humans.
