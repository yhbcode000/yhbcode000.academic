Point3 is a nonprofit software tool designed to help visually impaired individuals learn English through speech analysis and pronunciation feedback.

Built by the AierLab student team in 2022 after a father from Wuhan reached out for help — a commercial provider had quoted $30,000, far beyond his means. Our team delivered a functional tool with pronunciation evaluation, audio playback, and topic-based practice modules at no cost.

- **Year**: 2022
- **Role**: Project Lead
- **GitHub**: [point3-learning-tool](https://github.com/AierLab/point3-learning-tool)
